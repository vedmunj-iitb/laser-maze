# Life-size laser maze

A life-size 10 m x 10 m laser maze, built with diode lasers and photodetectors.

Techfest and Tinkerers' Laboratory, IIT Bombay, Sep 2023 to Dec 2023. Team of 60.

## What I did

- Led the 60-member team that built the maze.
- Raised INR 1.75 lakh from Techfest and the Institute Technical Council.

## The exhibit

Besides the lasers and photodetectors, the exhibit had step detection, glow-in-the-dark props
and a 1:500 scale model of the IIT Bombay campus.

It ran as a 2-day exhibit for 10,000+ visitors.
